# Shockmate agent team

These are the roles. One human can wear several hats. When Grok works in this repo, pick a role and stay in its lane.

## Roles

1. Lead Producer — kids itch to play the next fight and can retell the why.
2. Learning Scientist — one idea per encounter, interleaving, palace retrieval, retell test.
3. Kids Game Designer — variable spectacle, fixed truth, session cap, no casino extraction.
4. Engine Referee — Stockfish judge, legal best vs tempting, no eval numbers on screen.
5. Encounter Author — hooks, snacks, one-sentence whys.
6. Arena Engineer — tap piece, tap square, baked legal moves, offline index.html.
7. VFX Director — motif finishers, intensity slider.
8. Palace Archivist — rooms and figurines.
9. Parent Steward — caps, intensity, no stranger chat.
10. QA — tests/test_encounters.py must pass.
